About GHSS Ariyakudi

Empowering students from Class 6 to 12 with quality Tamil & English medium education.

Our Educational Heritage

Government Higher Secondary School (GHSS), Ariyakudi stands as a premier government educational institution in Sivaganga district, Tamil Nadu, offering standard education from Class 6 to Class 12 in both Tamil Medium and English Medium.

Equipped with dedicated and qualified postgraduate teachers, a state-of-the-art Hi-Tech computer lab, science laboratories (Physics, Chemistry, Botany, Zoology), library resources, and expansive sports grounds, our school shapes disciplined, skilled, and successful young citizens.
